Reception and lounge

The first thing you notice is the floor.

A Greek-key mosaic greets you at the door, in a city that began as the Greek colony of Tomis. From here the lift runs to the top floor, and an access ramp makes arriving easy with luggage or a pushchair.

The lounge

Tall windows, morning light and an à la carte breakfast menu. Later in the day, it is the place for a coffee or tea.

Good to know

Before you arrive.

What are the check-in times?

Check-in is from 15:00 to 20:30, and check-out is by 11:30. If you are arriving later, let reception know in advance.

Is there parking?

Yes. Private parking is free, subject to availability.

Is breakfast included?

Rates are for the room only. Breakfast is served à la carte in the lounge and paid for separately.

Can I cancel my booking?

Direct bookings can be cancelled free of charge at least 7 days before check-in. The exact terms are shown when you book.

Can I bring children?

Yes. Rooms can take an extra bed for children, and the rate depends on their age. Call us and we will find the right option.

Are pets allowed?

No, the hotel does not accept pets.

Can I smoke?

Rooms and indoor areas are non-smoking. There is a designated smoking area.

How can I pay?

By cash, card, bank transfer or Romanian holiday vouchers.
